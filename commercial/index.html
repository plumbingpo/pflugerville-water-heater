Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Pflugerville and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done after hours to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

A maintenance program from Pflugerville Water Heater is built around your property's specific equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include anode checks and tank flushing, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the tap. Hard water cuts the life of commercial equipment quickly, so busy properties often need quarterly visits.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
