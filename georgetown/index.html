How Georgetown Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your heater plays a big role. With regular service, a tank water heater typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, popping noises, or a damp spot around the base, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Georgetown

In Georgetown, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
